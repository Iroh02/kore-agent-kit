# Sample corpus - swap these files for whatever the organisers hand you

Drop .md / .txt / .csv / .json / .pdf into this folder and hit "Reindex" in
the UI. The content below is a small synthetic GCC fit-out contract so the
demo has something realistic to ground on.

## Payment terms

Interim payment applications are submitted on the 25th of each month.
The consultant certifies within 14 days and the employer pays certified
amounts within 30 days of certification. Late payment attracts interest at
2 percent above the prevailing EIBOR rate.

## Retention

Retention is deducted at 10 percent of each certified interim payment, up
to a cap of 5 percent of the contract sum. Half of the retention is released
on practical completion. The remaining half is released at the end of the
12 month defects liability period.

## Variations

No variation is valid unless instructed in writing by the consultant.
The contractor must submit the cost and time impact of an instructed
variation within 21 days of the instruction. A variation submitted after
21 days may be assessed by the consultant at their own valuation.
Dayworks rates apply only where no comparable BOQ rate exists.

## Delay and extension of time

Liquidated damages are 0.1 percent of the contract sum per day of delay,
capped at 10 percent of the contract sum. The contractor must give notice
of a delay event within 14 days of becoming aware of it, otherwise the
entitlement to an extension of time is lost.

## Workforce compliance

All site labour must be under a valid UAE work permit and paid through the
Wage Protection System by the 15th of the following month. Any worker whose
visa expires within 30 days must be flagged for renewal before mobilisation
to site.
